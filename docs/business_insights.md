# Executive Summary

The analysis shows a clear difference in OTT platform strategies.

Netflix dominates content scale with 5,240 titles and the highest
total IMDb engagement at approximately 220.38 million votes.

Disney+ demonstrates the strongest content quality and per-title
engagement, recording the highest average IMDb rating (6.65), the
highest average IMDb votes (118,574), and the highest proportion of
high-rated and highly popular content (31.8%).

Amazon Prime Video has a substantial catalog of 2,787 titles but
records lower average rating and engagement metrics compared with
Netflix and Disney+.

At the content level, 3,594 titles were identified as high-rated but
relatively low in popularity, representing a potential hidden-gem
opportunity for improved content discovery and promotion.

The analysis also identified 446 multi-platform titles, indicating
meaningful catalog overlap and competitive pressure across OTT
platforms.

---

# Key Business Insights

## 1. Platform Quality vs Scale

Disney+ recorded the highest average IMDb rating (6.65) and the
highest average IMDb votes per title (118,574), indicating strong
content quality and audience engagement on a per-title basis.

Netflix had the largest catalog with 5,240 titles and the highest
total IMDb engagement at approximately 220.38 million votes,
indicating a stronger advantage in content scale and overall reach.

### Business Takeaway

Disney+ appears to compete through content quality and engagement
per title, while Netflix's primary strength is catalog breadth and
overall audience reach.

---

## 2. High-Performing Content Efficiency

Disney+ had the highest proportion of content classified as both
high-rated and highly popular, with approximately 31.8% of its
titles meeting the project's high-performance criteria.

Netflix had the largest absolute number of high-performing titles
(715), but only 13.6% of its catalog fell into this category.

Amazon Prime Video had approximately 5.8% high-performing content.

### Business Takeaway

Disney+ demonstrates stronger high-performance content density,
while Netflix achieves greater absolute volume because of its
much larger catalog.

---

## 3. Hidden-Gem Opportunity

3,594 titles were classified as high-rated but relatively low in
IMDb engagement.

These titles represent a potential "hidden gem" segment: content
that demonstrates strong audience-rated quality but has comparatively
lower visibility or engagement.

### Business Takeaway

Platforms could potentially use recommendation systems,
personalized discovery, and targeted promotion to increase
visibility of high-quality under-engaged content.

---

## 4. High-Quality Genre Segments

Korean TV Shows achieved the highest average IMDb rating (7.50)
among genres containing at least 20 titles, followed by Classic
Movies (7.44) and Science & Nature TV (7.37).

### Business Takeaway

These genre categories demonstrate strong audience-rated quality
and could represent attractive areas for content acquisition,
curation, or recommendation strategies.

---

## 5. Platform Catalog Overlap

446 titles were identified as being available across multiple
OTT platforms.

### Business Takeaway

The overlap indicates meaningful competition for content and
suggests that platform differentiation depends not only on content
availability, but also on content quality, exclusivity, discovery,
and audience engagement.

---

## 6. Overall Content Benchmark

Across the analyzed OTT catalog, the average IMDb rating was 6.22
with approximately 38,226 IMDb votes per title.

### Business Takeaway

Platform and genre performance can be evaluated relative to this
overall benchmark rather than relying only on absolute ratings.
